What is an alert?

An alert means a month (or a forecast day) is at least {{ threshold }} °C hotter than normal for that time of year in that place. Normal = the average of the same calendar month: 1991–2020 for live alerts (ERA5, 6 major cities), 1951–1980 for the historical list (Berkeley Earth, Pakistan, 2001–2013).

Current threshold {{ threshold }} °C, {{ threshold_source }}. Administrators change it on the Admin page. Severity: Moderate below +2 °C · High +2 to +3 °C · Severe +3 °C or more.
